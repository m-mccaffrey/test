Build plan

Double Queen Platform Bed

Two queen mattresses side by side on one low platform frame: a 2× perimeter, a center spine under the mattress seam, a mid cross-beam, 2×4 joists, 4×4 legs and a ¾″ plywood deck. It aims for a 24″ mattress top, halfway between the current 29″ (too tall for the dog) and 19″ on the floor (too low for us).

    Notes & open questions

    • Mattress thickness sets everything. You said the base plus mattress is about 19″. The default here assumes a 9″ foundation and a 10″ mattress, which puts the deck at 14″. Measure the mattress by itself and enter it above.
    • It gets built in the bedroom. The finished frame is 10′ wide and won't fit through a door, and the 12′ rails won't go around some hallway corners. If yours won't make the turn, splice each head and foot rail over the center legs: cut two halves and add a 24″ scab of the same lumber, screwed and glued behind the joint.
    • It comes apart. It's all screws and hangers, with no glue in the frame, so you can take it down to move it. If the deck squeaks, add construction adhesive under the plywood, or a strip of felt on the tops of the joists.
    • The mattress seam. Two queens will drift apart over time. A foam "bed bridge" filler strip plus a king-split connector strap fixes that. For a raised lip that keeps the mattresses from sliding, screw 1×3 cleats around the deck edge. Take their height off your mattress-top math.
    • Dog access. At 24″ a medium dog can usually hop up. A small step or bench at the foot is an easy add-on later.
    • Floor clearance. Under the rails is open, which works for robot vacuums and storage bins. The number is under "Height stack" above.
